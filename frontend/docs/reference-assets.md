# Application artwork and reference evidence

The application uses original FinAI assets:

- `public/favicon.svg`: FinAI monogram, shared by the public navigation, footer, welcome screen, workspace and product previews through `BrandMark`.
- `public/visuals/finai-city.svg`: original geometric city illustration, authored for FinAI. It replaces the competitor-sourced photograph previously used in the hero.
- Company marks are generic Lucide icons paired with explicitly fictional demo companies. Integration tiles use category icons, not vendor wordmarks. The simulated integration catalog may identify products by name; it does not claim a partnership or live connection.

DM Sans and Space Grotesk are self-hosted from the Fontsource packages under their OFL-1.1 licenses. Lucide icons are distributed under the ISC license. Dependency versions and integrity hashes remain in the manifest and lockfile.

Attributed Rillet screenshots under `docs/reference/` remain comparison evidence only. They are not served as application artwork. The previous `public/visuals/city-reference.avif` has been removed from the application. Historical captures elsewhere in documentation may show that earlier background.

This inventory records provenance and implementation choices; it is not a legal clearance opinion.
